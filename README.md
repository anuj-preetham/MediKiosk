# MediKiosk — AI-Powered Patient Case-Taking Platform

[![Smart India Hackathon 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/SIH-2026-blue.svg)](https://sih.gov.in/)
[![Problem Statement](https://img.shields.io/badge/Problem%20ID-SIH26047-green.svg)](https://sih.gov.in/)
[![Organization](https://img.shields.io/badge/Ministry-Ayush%20%2F%20AIIA-orange.svg)](https://aiia.gov.in/)

**MediKiosk** is an AI-powered clinical history and medical-document intake platform developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem Statement ID: SIH26047)** for the **Ministry of Ayush / All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA)**.

It empowers patients to record comprehensive medical histories via natural voice conversation and guided touchscreens, digitizes physical prescriptions and lab reports, and generates structured, physician-ready summaries before consultation.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Multimodal Conversational Intake:** Adaptive voice + touch questioning in Hindi & English using the SOCRATES clinical framework.
2. **AYUSH Case-Taking:** Specialized assessment for *Dashavidha Pariksha* (Prakriti, Vikriti, Agni, etc.) and *Ahara-Vihara*.
3. **Medical Document Digitization & Timeline:** Multilingual OCR for handwritten/printed prescriptions & lab reports, with abnormal value highlighting and chronological history.
4. **Physician Review Dashboard:** Fast clinical interface for doctors to review, edit, confirm, or reject structured AI-generated histories.
5. **Red-Flag Triage:** Real-time detection and escalation of emergency symptom patterns to human medical staff.
6. **Consent, Privacy & ABDM Integration:** Granular consent, audio explanations, ABHA ID linkage, and FHIR interoperability.
